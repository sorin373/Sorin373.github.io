## Core idea

**Sample note to replace or expand.** Verification asks whether the system was built according to specified requirements. Validation asks whether the system solves the right problem for the intended use.

## Why it matters

In project documentation, mixing these terms makes evidence weaker. A vibration test can verify a structural requirement, while mission-level validation may require showing that the spacecraft concept can meet the user or mission need.

## Practical habit

For each requirement, keep a small chain of evidence: requirement, method, procedure, result and conclusion. If the result is not available yet, label the item as planned or pending.
